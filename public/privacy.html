Privacy policy

Effective: 9 September 2026

What the app processes

When you submit a prompt, AI ONE-TO-ALL sends the prompt and relevant conversation context to the AI providers you select through our server. The first release does not require an end-user account.

Local conversation history

Conversation history is stored in your device browser storage so you can reopen it. You can remove it using the Clear control in the app or by clearing the app's storage.

Advertising

The website uses Google AdSense and the Android app uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its partners may process cookies, device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other technical data to deliver, measure, secure, and personalize advertising where permitted. A consent message is shown when required before ads are requested. You can change eligible advertising privacy choices through Google's consent controls and your browser or device settings.

Service data

Our hosting provider may process basic technical data such as IP address, request time, device type, and error logs for security, reliability, and abuse prevention. We do not sell personal information.

AI providers

Selected AI providers process prompts to generate answers under their own terms and privacy practices. Avoid submitting passwords, financial details, private health records, or other sensitive information.

Children

The service is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian should supervise use by minors.

Contact and deletion

For privacy questions or deletion requests involving server logs, use the support contact listed in the app-store page. Device conversation history can be deleted directly in the app.

Changes

We may update this notice when the app or its providers change. The effective date above will be updated.
